THE COMPLETE PATH

A reactor supplies the heat.

Hydrogen carries that heat away and leaves as a fast exhaust. The solid nuclear fuel stays inside the engine; hydrogen is the expelled propellant.

Read left to right through the core. The lower loop returns cooling hydrogen to the inlet.

What the section reveals

The outer pressure vessel contains the reactor. A surrounding reflector helps retain neutrons. The exposed longitudinal elements represent solid fuel containing hydrogen passages. Support plates hold the core; the nozzle converts hot-gas energy into exhaust motion.

Conceptual, not to scale. The fuel-element count, dimensions and route are illustrative. This simplified serial cooling route omits turbine-drive branches, valves, control drums, shielding and detailed manifolds. No temperature, thrust or reactor-control simulation is implied. Forward thrust is opposite the rightward exhaust.
